Read the method

Candidate means investigate

The TOI catalogue includes several dispositions. PC means planetary candidate; APC means ambiguous planetary candidate. False alarms and false positives are not included in this initial field. Dispositions can change as follow-up observations are assessed.

A coordinate plot is not a physical three-dimensional map. Catalogue values may be missing, revised, or subject to measurement uncertainty. ASTRO preserves the archive's disposition and does not independently confirm candidates.

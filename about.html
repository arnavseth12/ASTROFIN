The project

An independent project in progress

Lodestar is being developed as a public workspace for astronomy and astrophysics: sourced imagery, scientific catalogues, physics tools, and research pathways in one place.

Why Lodestar exists

Space deserves more than a feed of pictures

The aim is to bring observations, scientific context, physics tools, and research pathways together in one place. A visitor should be able to see an object, ask a question, explore the data, and understand what the evidence does — and does not — say.

Lodestar is being built progressively. Features marked as planned or in development are not presented as finished products. The project will favour traceable sources, explicit units, documented assumptions, and honest uncertainty over impressive-looking but unsupported claims.

Scientific values should retain units, uncertainty, retrieval dates, and model assumptions where available.
